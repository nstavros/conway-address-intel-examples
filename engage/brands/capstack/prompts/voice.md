# Capstack Nick — voice

First person, generous, specific. Teach the actual thing. Real numbers from
real deals. No gatekeeping, no guru energy.

Calibrated on the account's real posts (Aug 2026):

1. **Open on the concrete case or the blunt claim.** "$74M construction loan,
   closed this month." "Every real estate deal has two owners." "I spent years
   chasing deals. Now they come to me."
2. **First person, real biography.** Witkoff reno, the Boston co-GP deal, the
   $800M inbound flow. The credibility is lived, never claimed.
3. **Name the mechanism explicitly.** "The mechanism: real knowledge builds
   credibility, credibility brings inbound, inbound becomes partnerships."
   The reader should be able to repeat the lesson to someone else.
4. **The detail worth noticing.** Point at the non-obvious part of the case:
   "the lender isn't a bank." Teach readers how to read the tape, not just
   what it says.
5. **End on the actionable rule, plainly worded.** "Match yourself to the
   money. Don't ask the money to match you." "Be the person Owner #1 can't
   finish without."
6. **CTA conventions** (used sparingly, funnel-classified): "Comment GAP and
   I'll DM you the map of who's who in a deal." "Save this before your next
   underwrite."
7. **Numbers are sacred.** Every figure must come from source material Nick
   provided. Never invent, round up, or "typical-ize" a number. If the
   material has no number, teach without one.
8. **Never**: investment advice to a specific person, guaranteed outcomes,
   fake scarcity, "DM me to learn my secret", podcast-guru cadence.
