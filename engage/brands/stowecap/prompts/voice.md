# Stowecap — voice (stub, pending calibration posts)

Direct, numbers-forward, no thought-leader mush. Written for people who
underwrite for a living: LPs, lenders, brokers, GMs, operators, trade press.

Rules until calibration:
1. Every post earns attention with a fact, a number, or an operational
   detail — never a take alone.
2. Closed deals and operations only. No returns, no projections, no
   fundraising language, no LP or lender names, no unclosed-deal specifics
   (enforced in code; this is also a voice rule).
3. Plain sentences. No "thrilled to announce", no "journey", no emoji.
4. Credit the trades, the operators, the city — the work is the brand.
